# related work

one-liners from published papers on pii masking, de-identification, synthetic data and re-identification, sorted by lab and by how much they bear on maskara. crawled 2026-10-27.

- **351 papers**, after duplicates were removed. each row is the paper's core idea in a line, plus what it means for maskara.
- **verification:** all 228 arxiv links were opened and their titles matched to the cited paper. all 63 pubmed ids were looked up in ncbi and matched. the other links were checked to load; some publisher sites (ieee, acm, ssrn, harvard) block automated requests, so those were not opened. lab names come from author lists and abstracts, not pdf headers, and rows marked `*` or "unverified" are a guess.
- **coverage:** the crawl hit a web-search cap partway through, so finance, legal, and some labs are thin. see [gaps](#gaps-and-open-niches).

## gaps and open niches

**an exact match was not established by this crawl; novelty is unproven.** the search was incomplete and does not establish the absence of prior work on keyed, consistent surrogates across documents, tables and repeated runs. HIPS, patient-centered pseudonymization and LOPSIDED already cover important parts of the approach. A methods paper needs a precise comparison and measured improvement over strong baselines, not a claim that this combination has never existed.

**commercial overlap matters too.** Tonic documents consistency between selected replacement values in Textual and Structural, including a shared statistics seed across runs or databases ([official documentation](https://docs.tonic.ai/textual/dataset-configure-redaction/textual-datasets-synthesis-options/enable-structural-consistency.md), checked 2026-10-27). Cross-format consistency alone is not an established differentiator. Maskara's next evaluation isolates replacement rendering from detection, date handling and entity resolution; see [bench results](bench-results.md).

**cross-modal identity consistency remains a research question in this review.** this crawl did not establish an end-to-end comparison for mapping one person to a synthetic identity across text, face and voice; it does not prove no such work exists. Related pieces include RiDDLE (keyed fake faces), video identity-consistency papers and pseudo-speaker voice work. Multimodal breadth is deferred until text/table utility and risk can be measured against stronger controls.

**date shifting has known attacks.** Shift and Truncate is the formal reference. the 2023 and 2026 papers show weekday and seasonal patterns can expose a shift.

**weak coverage:**
- finance: only two J.P. Morgan papers and one on financial-document pii were confirmed. nothing turned up from Capital One, Bloomberg, Mastercard, Visa, or Amex, and nothing on synthetic transactions (PaySim, IBM AML).
- legal: three court-ruling papers plus TAB and the Korean judgment datasets.
- labs: little from Stanford, CMU, UW/AI2, Princeton, CISPA, Kakao, Samsung, Upstage, NTT, Baidu, or ByteDance.
- topics: contextual-integrity benchmarks (ConfAIde, PrivacyLens) and the SDMetrics, SyntheRela, and ENISA pseudonymisation reports.

**korean, japanese, and chinese:** pii datasets are mostly clinical or legal, with no banking data. voice anonymization is tested almost only on english and mandarin.
